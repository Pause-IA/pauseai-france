## Job Offers

### Communications Officer (fixed-term contract, 60%)

Pause IA is hiring a Communications Officer to structure and amplify its communications, grow its media presence and build up a team of volunteers engaged on these missions.

- **Contract**: 12-month fixed-term contract
- **Working time**: 60%, i.e. 21 hours per week
- **Salary**: €25,000 gross per year
- **Location**: remote, with occasional travel within France
- **Start date**: as soon as possible
- **Applications**: until 6 September, at [contact@pauseia.fr](mailto:contact@pauseia.fr)

This is a French-speaking role based in France, and the full job description is available in French.

**[Read the full offer in French](/fr/recrutement-emploi)**
